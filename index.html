About SARAL SHIKSHYA

Established in 2015, SARAL SHIKSHYA has been a pioneer in providing quality bridge courses for SEE graduates in Kathmandu. Our mission is to make quality education accessible and help students achieve their academic dreams.

With a team of dedicated educators and a proven curriculum, we've helped hundreds of students secure admissions in prestigious institutions like St. Xavier's, Budhanilkantha, SOS, Rato Bangala, and international A-Level programs.
